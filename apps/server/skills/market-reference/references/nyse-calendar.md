# NYSE calendar 2026–2027

Source (verified October 24, 2026): ICE press release of November 8, 2024, https://ir.theice.com/press/news-details/2024/NYSE-Group-Announces-2025-2026-and-2027-Holiday-and-Early-Closings-Calendar/default.aspx, and https://www.nyse.com/markets/hours-calendars.

Regular session 9:30 a.m.–4:00 p.m. ET. Early closes end at 1:00 p.m. ET.

## 2026 full closures (verified)

Jan 1 (New Year's Day), Jan 19 (MLK Day), Feb 16 (Presidents Day), Apr 3 (Good Friday), May 25 (Memorial Day), Jun 19 (Juneteenth), Jul 3 (Independence Day observed), Sep 7 (Labor Day), Nov 26 (Thanksgiving), Dec 25 (Christmas).

## 2026 early closes, 1:00 p.m. ET (verified)

Nov 27 (day after Thanksgiving), Dec 24 (Christmas Eve).

## 2027 full closures (verified)

Jan 1 (New Year's Day), Jan 18 (MLK Day), Feb 15 (Presidents Day), Mar 26 (Good Friday), May 31 (Memorial Day), Jun 18 (Juneteenth observed), Jul 5 (Independence Day observed), Sep 6 (Labor Day), Nov 25 (Thanksgiving), Dec 24 (Christmas observed).

## 2027 early closes, 1:00 p.m. ET (verified)

Nov 26 (day after Thanksgiving). No Christmas Eve early close is listed for 2027 because Christmas falls on a Saturday and the Friday itself is the observed closure.

## Trading implications

- No entries in the last 15 minutes of any session; on early-close days treat 12:30 p.m. ET as the effective close for new entries.
- Holiday-shortened weeks have thin volume: demand the full 1.5x volume confirmation on breakouts and skip entries where the spread gate fails.
