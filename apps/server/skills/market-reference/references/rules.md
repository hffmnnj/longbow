# Trading rules

Sources:
- PDT removal: https://www.finra.org/rules-guidance/notices/26-10 (Regulatory Notice 26-10, published April 20, 2026) and https://www.federalregister.gov/d/2026-07485 (SEC order). Verified October 24, 2026.
- T+1, good-faith violations, wash sales: standard rules summarized below; confirm current text at https://www.sec.gov and https://www.irs.gov. Marked unverified.

## Pattern day trader rule (verified)

- The SEC approved FINRA's amendments to Rule 4210 on April 14, 2026 (Release No. 105226), replacing day-trading margin provisions with an intraday margin standard.
- Eliminated: the "pattern day trader" definition, day-trade counting for designation, the $25,000 minimum equity requirement, and day-trading buying-power calculations.
- New model: members compute an intraday margin deficit for margin accounts with same-day risk-increasing activity; deficits must be satisfied promptly (by close of business on the 5th business day at the latest for repeat offenders, or a 90-calendar-day freeze on increasing risk applies). Small deficits (under the lesser of 5 percent of equity or $1,000) are exempt from the practice-of-failure finding.
- Effective June 4, 2026. Members needing more time may phase in implementation over 18 months, until October 20, 2027.
- Practical effect for this system: keep the internal cap of 3 day trades in 5 business days until the broker confirms cutover. The system's minimum 1-day hold already keeps day trades rare.

## T+1 settlement (unverified; confirm at sec.gov)

- US stock trades settle one business day after the trade date (since May 28, 2024).
- In cash accounts, selling then reusing unsettled proceeds before settlement is a good-faith violation; repeated violations (commonly 3 in 12 months) bring a 90-day restriction to settled-cash-only trading.

## Wash sales (unverified; confirm at irs.gov Publication 550)

- Selling at a loss and buying a substantially identical security within 30 days before or after disallows the loss for tax purposes.
- The disallowed amount adds to the basis of the replacement shares; track adjusted basis in the journal so R-multiples reflect economics, not tax lots.
