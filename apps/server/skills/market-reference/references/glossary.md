# Glossary

- ATR: average true range, the standard per-share volatility unit. Stops and position sizes are denominated in ATR multiples.
- R-multiple: trade P&L divided by initial dollar risk. A +$200 gain on $100 risk is 2R.
- MFE / MAE: maximum favorable / adverse excursion of a trade in R while open. Used to tune exits and entries.
- Brier score: mean squared error between stated win probabilities and binary outcomes. Lower is better calibrated.
- Relative strength: a name's return minus SPY's return over the same window, in percentage points. Not the RSI indicator.
- Base: a sideways consolidation under resistance before a breakout. Tight means a under-12-percent range over 20 closes.
- Drift: the tendency for post-earnings gaps to continue in the gap direction over subsequent sessions.
- Invalidation: the price at which the trade thesis is wrong and the position must exit. Every order carries one.
- Heat: total open risk across positions as a fraction of equity. Ceiling 3 percent.
- Blackout: a window around an event (earnings, macro releases) where new entries are barred.
- Good-faith violation: spending unsettled sale proceeds in a cash account before settlement.
- Wash sale: loss disallowed for tax when repurchasing a substantially identical security within 30 days either side.
- Intraday margin deficit (FINRA Rule 4210 as amended 2026): the peak same-day shortfall between required margin and account equity after risk-increasing activity. Replaces the old day-trade counting framework.
- SEP: the FOMC's Summary of Economic Projections, released with the dot plot at March, June, September, and December meetings.
- 2s10s / 3m10y: Treasury yield curve spreads. Inversion and rapid re-steepening both carry regime information.
